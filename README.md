# 📈 Adani Stock Data Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Stock Market Dashboard** built using Adani stock price data. The dashboard analyzes historical stock performance through multiple KPIs, including **Open Price, Close Price, 52-Week High & Low, Trading Volume, and Quarterly Trends**. Interactive filters enable users to explore stock performance across different years, quarters, months, and days.

The objective is to help investors and business analysts identify market trends, monitor price movements, analyze trading activity, and support data-driven investment decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Stock prices fluctuate daily due to market sentiment, trading activity, and economic events. Investors need an intuitive dashboard that consolidates key stock performance indicators into a single view for faster analysis and better decision-making.

This dashboard addresses that need by providing interactive visualizations for:

* Historical stock price trends
* Trading volume analysis
* Quarterly performance comparison
* 52-week High & Low analysis
* Daily Open vs Close price movement
* Month-wise trading activity

---

# 📊 Dashboard KPIs

| KPI                      | Description                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Average 52-Week High** | Tracks the average highest stock price over the previous 52 weeks.  |
| **Average 52-Week Low**  | Displays the average lowest stock price over the previous 52 weeks. |
| **Total Trading Volume** | Measures total shares traded during the selected period.            |
| **Open Total**           | Aggregate opening stock prices.                                     |
| **Close Total**          | Aggregate closing stock prices.                                     |

# 📈 Executive Summary

* The stock consistently trades within a defined price range, with the **52-week high remaining well above the current market price**, indicating potential upside while reflecting market volatility.
* **Quarter 2 records the strongest overall price levels**, whereas **Quarter 4 shows comparatively weaker performance**, suggesting seasonal fluctuations in market activity.
* **Trading volume peaks in April**, indicating increased investor participation during this period, while later months experience lower market activity.
* The **Open and Close prices remain closely aligned** throughout the analysis period, reflecting relatively stable daily price movements with limited intraday volatility.
* Daily stock prices exhibit periodic fluctuations but largely remain within the broader yearly trading range, suggesting consistent market behavior rather than extreme price swings.

# 📊 Business Insights

## 1. KPI Analysis

### Observation

* Average **52-Week High** ranges between **1.53K and 1.85K**.
* Average **52-Week Low** varies between **995 and 1.29K**.
* Trading volume reaches as high as **322M shares** during broader selections.
* Open and Close prices remain nearly identical across most periods.

### Business Interpretation

The small difference between opening and closing prices indicates relatively balanced trading sessions with limited intraday volatility. Significant variation in trading volume suggests that investor participation changes considerably depending on the selected period.

### Recommendation

Monitor periods with unusually high trading volume, as they often precede significant price movements or major market events.

---

## 2. Quarterly 52-Week High vs Low

### Observation

Quarter 2 consistently records the highest combined 52-week High and Low values, while Quarter 4 records the lowest.

### Business Interpretation

Quarter 2 appears to represent stronger market confidence and higher valuations, whereas Quarter 4 reflects comparatively weaker pricing levels.

### Business Impact

Seasonal patterns may influence investor behavior and portfolio performance.

### Recommendation

Analyze historical business announcements and macroeconomic events occurring during Quarter 2 to understand the drivers of stronger performance.

---

## 3. Daily Stock Price Trend

### Observation

Daily Open and Close prices move closely together, with only moderate day-to-day fluctuations.

### Business Interpretation

The stock demonstrates relatively stable short-term trading behavior without excessive daily volatility.

### Business Impact

Stable price movement may appeal to long-term investors seeking reduced market risk.

### Recommendation

Continue monitoring price movements alongside market news to identify potential breakout opportunities.

---

## 4. Daily Open vs Close Price

### Observation

Opening and Closing prices remain highly correlated across weekdays.

### Business Interpretation

The market generally prices the stock efficiently, with limited differences between opening and closing sessions.

### Business Impact

Reduced intraday volatility lowers short-term trading risk but may limit opportunities for aggressive day trading.

### Recommendation

Combine technical indicators with price analysis to improve short-term trading strategies.

---

## 5. Monthly Trading Volume

### Observation

April records the highest trading volume, while July and December experience comparatively lower market participation.

### Business Interpretation

Higher trading volume indicates increased investor interest and liquidity during certain months.

### Business Impact

Periods of high trading activity often coincide with earnings announcements, corporate events, or broader market movements.

### Recommendation

Focus investment analysis around high-volume periods, as they typically provide stronger market signals.

---

## 6. Monthly 52-Week High, Low & Volume

### Observation

Monthly trading volume fluctuates while the 52-week High and Low remain relatively stable.

### Business Interpretation

Price stability alongside changing trading volume suggests that increased market participation does not always translate into significant price changes.

### Business Impact

Higher volume without large price swings may indicate accumulation or distribution by institutional investors.

### Recommendation

Use volume trends together with technical indicators to identify potential trend reversals.

# ⚠️ Risks & Opportunities

## Risks

* Trading volume declines in several months, potentially reducing market liquidity.
* The stock remains below its 52-week high, indicating incomplete price recovery.

## Opportunities

* High-volume months provide stronger signals for investment decisions.
* Stable daily price movement supports long-term investment strategies.
* Seasonal analysis can improve portfolio timing and entry decisions.
* Comparing price trends with corporate announcements may identify performance drivers.
